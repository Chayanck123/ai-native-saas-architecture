Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Got questions about integrations, security, or guarantees? We've got answers.

How does PolicyPilot guarantee 0% hallucinations? ▼
Unlike raw ChatGPT models which guess when uncertain, PolicyPilot uses a dual-engine architecture: every draft answer is audited by an internal Critic model against your exact indexed policy text. If the answer cannot be cited directly from your policy documents, the assistant does not bluff—it politely escalates the customer's request to your human team.
How difficult is it to install on Shopify or WooCommerce? ▼
It takes under 60 seconds. You copy a single line of script code from your dashboard and paste it right before the closing </body> tag in your Shopify theme.liquid file. No coding or complex app installation required.
Can I cancel or change my plan anytime? ▼
Yes, absolutely. There are no contracts, lock-ins, or hidden cancellation fees. You can cancel, downgrade, or upgrade your subscription with one click directly inside your Merchant Dashboard.
Is our store data and customer chat private? ▼
100% private. We use isolated multi-tenant architecture with row-level encryption. Your customer chats and policy documents are never used to train global public AI models.
What happens when my 14-day free pilot ends? ▼
You get 50 automated answers completely free for 14 days without entering a credit card. When your trial ends, you can choose to upgrade to our $79/mo Starter plan to keep your assistant live, or your assistant will automatically pause without charging you a cent.

Your Store's Embed Code

Add this script to your Shopify theme or website to activate the floating support bubble.

<script 
  src="https://getpolicypilot.vercel.app/widget.js" 
  data-store-id="YOUR_STORE_SLUG" 
  data-theme="dark" 
  async>

Shopify 1-Minute Installation Instructions:

  1. In your Shopify Admin, go to Online Store ➔ Themes.
  2. Click ... ➔ Edit code next to your active theme.
  3. Open theme.liquid and scroll to the very bottom.
  4. Paste the code snippet right before the closing </body> tag and click Save.
