Can I pay using UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm? ▼
Yes! We support all Indian payment methods including instant UPI QR code scanning, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, CRED, NetBanking, and Indian business credit/debit cards. You also receive a GST-compliant invoice for input tax credit.
How does PolicyPilot guarantee 0% hallucinations? ▼
Unlike raw ChatGPT models which guess when uncertain, PolicyPilot uses a dual-engine architecture: every draft answer is audited by an internal Critic model against your exact indexed policy text. If the answer cannot be cited directly from your policy documents, the assistant does not bluff—it politely escalates the customer's request to your human team.
How difficult is it to install on Shopify or WooCommerce? ▼
It takes under 60 seconds. You copy a single line of script code from your dashboard and paste it right before the closing </body> tag in your Shopify theme.liquid file. No complex coding required.
Can I cancel or change my plan anytime? ▼
Yes, absolutely. There are no long-term lock-ins or hidden cancellation fees. You can cancel, downgrade, or upgrade your subscription with one click directly inside your Merchant Dashboard.
What happens when my 14-day free pilot ends? ▼
You get 50 automated answers completely free for 14 days without entering a card or UPI ID. When your trial ends, you can choose to activate your plan to keep your assistant live, or it will simply pause with zero obligation.
